Setup Instructions

Mobile Setup:

  1. Bookmark this page on your mobile device
  2. Add this bookmarklet to your bookmarks: Add to LinkSync (drag to your bookmarks bar on desktop or create a bookmark on mobile)
  3. When browsing Twitter/Reddit and you find a link, tap your browser's bookmark button and select the LinkSync bookmarklet

Alternative Share Method:

  1. When on a page you want to save, use your device's share function
  2. Choose "Copy link" or "Copy to clipboard"
  3. Visit this page and paste the link - it will be automatically saved
